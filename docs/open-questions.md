| OQ-08 | data-model.md `units`: `UNIQUE (property_id, label)` | Does an archived unit's label still block a new unit with the same label? | Assumption | Yes. The constraint covers archived units too, so the history of "Unit A" is never ambiguous. Unarchiving is the way back. |
| OQ-09 | FR-03 | A unit can't be archived with an active lease. What about archiving a property? | Assumption | Same rule: a property can't be archived while any of its units has an active lease. |
| OQ-10 | FR-05, FR-17 | What happens when an active lease passes its end date and no move-out is recorded? | Assumption | It stays `active` and gets no charges after its end date. Holdover or month-to-month rent is not in v1. The lease page says "Past end date: record the move-out or renew". |
| OQ-11 | FR-10 | Is the payment reference required? Cash often has none. | Assumption | Optional. When present it must be unique within the lease (D-24). |
| OQ-12 | US-11, FR-09 | A lease starting 2026-11-16 with due day 1 would get a November due date before the tenant moved in. | Assumption | The first month's due date is the later of the due day and the start date (D-23). |
| OQ-13 | data-model.md `refunds.status`; NFR-04 | The data model stores a refund `status` that changes from `pending` to `paid`, which sits awkwardly with "statuses are derived". | Resolved | A `refunds` row records the refund being paid and is never edited. `pending` means an ended lease holds a deposit and has no refund row yet (D-20). |
| OQ-14 | US-18, FR-15 | Can deductions be recorded while the lease is still active? | Assumption | No. Deductions, and the refund, are recorded once the lease has ended. A deposit can't be added after the lease ends, or after the refund is paid. |
