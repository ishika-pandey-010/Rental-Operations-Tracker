# Discovery notes (S0)

Five short conversations to confirm or cut problems P1–P5 and assumptions A1–A4 in the [PRD](PRD.md). **Not run yet.** Record participants by code only (D1–D5).

## Who to talk to

People who manage 1–20 rental units themselves, or part-time managers for friends or family. Sources: landlord forums and groups, people you know who rent out a room or flat.

## Guide (20 minutes)

1. How many units do you look after, and how do you track rent today? Show me if you can.
2. Walk me through last month: from rent being due to knowing everyone paid.
3. Tell me about the last time someone paid only part of the rent. What did you do to track the rest? *(P2, A2)*
4. Have you ever recorded the same payment twice, or chased someone who had already paid? *(P1)*
5. Where do you keep track of deposits? How did the last refund go? *(P3, P4)*
6. When someone moves in or out mid-month, how do you work out what they owe? *(P5, Q1)*
7. Do you record payments when they arrive or in a batch later? *(A1)*
8. Do you ever deal in more than one currency? *(A4)*
9. If a tool did this for you, what would make you trust its numbers?

Ask for stories about the last time it happened, not opinions about what they'd want. "Would you use…?" questions predict little.
