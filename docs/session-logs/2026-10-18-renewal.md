# Renewing a lease, 2026-10-18

## What the user needs
A good tenant wants another year. The landlord wants to set up the new term, maybe at a new rent, without retyping the tenant and unit, and without having to refund the deposit only to take it straight back.

## What the docs said
- FR-18 (Should), US-08 ([user-stories.md](../user-stories.md#us-08-renew-a-lease)): a new lease linked to the old one, which ends on its end date (AC1); "carry deposit over" puts the deposit on the new lease with no refund on the old one (AC2).
- D-10: a renewal is a new lease, not an edit.

## Plan
A `carried_from_lease_id` column on `deposits`, a new definition of "held" (received minus carried out), `renew_lease()`, a collapsed Renew form on active leases, and links between the two leases. Left out: renewing an ended lease (a returning tenant is a new lease), and renewal with a different unit or due day.

## Tests written
Database (`tests/db/renewal.test.ts`, 6 tests). Before the migration all 6 failed with `function renew_lease(...) does not exist`.
- TC-LEASE-10, TC-LEASE-11.
- Mine: without carry-over the old lease's refund goes pending and the new lease stays a draft; October is billed on the old lease at the old rent and November on the new one at the new rent; a start date on or before the old end is refused and leaves the old lease active; an ended lease can't be renewed.

End-to-end (`e2e/renewal.spec.ts`): TC-LEASE-10, including both links, and TC-LEASE-11, including the Pending refunds report.

## What I built
- `supabase/migrations/20261018170000_renewal.sql`. `renew_lease()` creates the new lease with `create_lease()`, links it, ends the old lease with `move_out()` on its own end date (so nothing is prorated or adjusted), and when carrying over writes one deposit row on the new lease and activates it. Because "held" now subtracts deposits carried out, the old lease holds 0 and `lease_refund_status` reports `nothing_held`, so it never reaches Pending refunds.
- Lease page: Renew section with start (day after the current end), end (one year on) and rent (the current rent) filled in, and "Carry the $1,200.00 deposit over" ticked; "Renewal of…" and "Renewed by…" links in Terms; deposit notes shown under each deposit row.

## User review
- Main task: renew for another year at the same rent, keeping the deposit.
- Steps: click "Renew this lease", click Renew lease. 2 clicks, nothing typed; every field is pre-filled with the common case. A rent rise is one more field. The landlord lands on the new lease page, already active, with the deposit shown.
- Without carry-over the confirmation says what's left to do ("Record a deposit or a waiver, then activate it").
- A failure that was my test's fault: both renewal tests first failed because my label regex expected "Carry the deposit" but the label includes the amount ("Carry the $1,200.00 deposit over…").
- Remaining friction: renewing early ends the old lease straight away in status terms, even though it still runs to its end date. Its October charge is still generated (D-27), but the Leases list shows it as Ended a month early. That's a side effect of D-10 with only three statuses; I've left it and noted it here.
