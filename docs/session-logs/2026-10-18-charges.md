# Monthly charges and first-month proration, 2026-10-18

## What the user needs
On the 1st of the month the landlord wants every tenant's rent on the books in one action, with mid-month move-ins charged only for the days they lived there, and no chance of billing a month twice.

## What the docs said
- FR-08, US-10 ([user-stories.md](../user-stories.md#us-10-generate-monthly-charges)): one click per month; running it again creates nothing and says "0 new charges, 3 already existed"; two runs at the same moment still give one charge per lease; drafts aren't charged.
- FR-09, US-11: a 2026-11-16 start at 1,200.00 gives 600.00 (15 of 30 days).
- D-04 (actual days, half-up), D-06 (partial unique index plus `ON CONFLICT DO NOTHING`), R-01, R-05.

## Plan
`charges` (append-only), the proration functions, `generate_rent_charges(period)`, and a Charges page. Left out on purpose: `charge_adjustments` (created in the move-out branch, where it first gets rows, so the RLS suite's "every table has a row" check stays honest), balances and status (payments branch), and credit auto-apply (payments branch).

## What I built
- `supabase/migrations/20261018100000_charges.sql`. `prorate_cents` is integer half-up: `(rent × days × 2 + dim) ÷ (2 × dim)`. `occupied_days` and `days_in_month` feed it. `rent_for_period` reads the rent schedule (D-05). `rent_due_date` applies D-23. `lock_lease` is the per-lease advisory lock (D-26). `generate_rent_charges` loops over leases that cover the month, including ended leases up to their move-out (D-27), and inserts with `ON CONFLICT … DO NOTHING`.
- A prorated charge's description says so: "Rent November 2026. Prorated: 15 of 30 days". The landlord can then answer the tenant's "why is it 600?" from the screen.
- `src/app/(app)/charges/…`: month picker defaulting to the current month, Generate rent, and the month's charges. "Charges" added to the nav.

## User review
- Main task: charge December's rent for every active lease.
- Steps: open Charges, click Generate rent. 1 click, 1 screen; the month is already set to the current month. Nothing to remove here, so no before/after change.
- Feedback: the button reads "Generating…" while it runs, then the page shows "2 new charges, 0 already existed" and the month's charges below it. Running it again is harmless and says so, which matters more to a landlord who isn't sure whether they already did it.
- First use: with no active leases the page says "No active leases to charge yet" with a link to Leases, instead of offering a button that would do nothing.
- Keyboard: the month field and button are reachable in order; Enter in the month field submits.
- Browsers: Safari desktop has no month picker and shows a text box. Typing `2026-12` works because the server only checks the `YYYY-MM` shape. Not tested in Safari itself; that's TC-NFR-09 for UAT.
- Remaining friction: generating is a separate visit to Charges. Putting "Generate this month's rent" on the home page belongs with the dashboard (US-23), so it's left for S3.
