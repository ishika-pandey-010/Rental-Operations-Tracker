# Overdue rent and pending refunds, 2026-10-18

## What the user needs
Two questions a landlord asks most weeks: who do I need to chase today, and whose deposit am I still sitting on?

## What the docs said
- FR-19, US-20 ([user-stories.md](../user-stories.md#us-20-overdue-rent)): tenant, unit, balance and days overdue for each charge past due (AC1); paid and not-yet-due charges left out (AC2, AC3).
- FR-20, US-21: ended leases with an unpaid refund, with the amount and days since the end (AC1); gone once paid (AC2).
- PRD Q3 / OQ-05: overdue from the day after the due date. OQ-19: "today" comes from the caller. uat-plan.md §4: report dates are fixed for UAT, not waited for.

## Plan
Two SQL functions taking an `as_of` date, two pages with an "As of" field, and two nav links. TC-NFR-05 (the monthly routine by keyboard) also goes in here, because Overdue is its last step. Left out: CSV export (US-22, next-but-one branch). I briefly added "Export CSV" buttons pointing at routes that don't exist yet, then took them out before committing.

## Tests written
Database (`tests/db/reports.test.ts`, 12 tests). Before the migration all 12 failed with `function overdue_rent(unknown) does not exist` or `function pending_refunds(unknown) does not exist`.
- TC-RPT-01 to 05.
- Mine: empty first use; a charge due today isn't overdue but is the next day; a charge cancelled by a move-out isn't listed; oldest first; an unpaid "Deductions above deposit" charge is listed; another landlord's charges never show; an active lease with a deposit isn't a pending refund; a refund due of 0.00 still lists until the landlord closes it.

End-to-end (`e2e/reports.spec.ts`): TC-RPT-01 to 05, the empty Overdue state, and TC-NFR-05. The keyboard test goes from the home page to Charges, generates December, opens the lease from the charges table, records 500.00, checks the focused field has a visible outline, then reaches Overdue, all with Tab, typing and Enter.

## What I built
- `supabase/migrations/20261018150000_reports.sql`: `overdue_rent(as_of)` reads `charge_balances`; `pending_refunds(as_of)` reads `lease_refund_status`. Both are `security invoker`, so RLS still decides what a landlord sees.
- `/reports/overdue` with a total row; `/reports/refunds` with held, deductions and refund due. Both use a plain GET form for the date, so a report for a given date is a link that can be bookmarked or sent.

## User review
- Main task: see who is behind today.
- Steps: click Overdue in the nav. 1 click, 1 screen. Each tenant name links to their lease, where the payment form is, so chasing then recording is 2 more clicks.
- Feedback and empty states: "Nobody is behind as of 2026-12-11" and "No refunds waiting as of …" instead of an empty table.
- Keyboard: the routine works without a mouse (TC-NFR-05 passes). Getting from the lease page to the nav takes many Tab presses because the ledger's Reverse controls are all tab stops. The skip link helps going down, not back up. Not changed.
- Phone: with seven nav links the nav row scrolls sideways inside itself; the page doesn't. The phone-width test still passes.
- Remaining friction: no reminder pushes these reports at the landlord; they have to look. Email reminders need an email service and aren't in any story.
