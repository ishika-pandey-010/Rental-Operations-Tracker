# Early move-out, 2026-10-18

## What the user needs
A tenant hands back the keys on 10 February, partway through a lease. The landlord wants February charged only for the ten days, nothing charged after, and any rent already paid for the rest of the month kept on record as the tenant's money, without working any of it out by hand.

## What the docs said
- FR-17, FR-09, US-09 ([user-stories.md](../user-stories.md#us-09-early-move-out)): move-out 2027-02-10 gives 428.57 (AC1); if February was already charged, a −771.43 adjustment is added and the original stays (AC2); March gets nothing (AC3).
- data-model.md: `charge_adjustments`, "adjustment = prorated amount − original charge".
- D-03, NFR-02: corrections are new rows.

## Plan
`charge_adjustments`, `move_out(lease, date)`, adjustment rows in the ledger, and a Move-out section on active leases. Left out: undoing a move-out. The lifecycle trigger forbids ended → active, so a wrong move-out date can't be fixed in the app (noted below).

## Tests written
Database (`tests/db/moveout.test.ts`, 12 tests). Before the migration all 12 failed with `function move_out(...) does not exist`.
- TC-LEASE-12, 13, 14.
- Mine: February already paid in full (771.43 released to credit, February stays paid); March charged in advance gets cancelled (value 0, status `cancelled`); released money pays an older unpaid month first; a move-out on the last day needs no adjustment; a date after the lease ends is refused; a draft can't be moved out; an adjustment by SQL that would leave a charge worth less than was paid is refused; adjustments can't be edited; the ledger shows the adjustment on the move-out date and still ties out.

End-to-end (`e2e/moveout.spec.ts`): TC-LEASE-13, TC-LEASE-12 with TC-LEASE-14, and an out-of-range date.

## What I built
- `supabase/migrations/20261018130000_move_out.sql`. `charge_value_cents()` now adds adjustments; nothing else had to change for balances, the caps or credit. `enforce_adjustment_rules()` keeps value ≥ 0 and value ≥ allocated. `release_down_to(charge, keep)` releases the newest payment's money first. `move_out()` ends the lease, adjusts the move-out month and any later charged months, then runs `apply_credit()`.
- Lease page: a Move-out section for active leases, with the date defaulting to today clamped to the lease term and a plain explanation of what will happen; "Moved out …" in Terms; a "Past end date" banner for active leases past their end date (OQ-10).

## User review
- Main task: record that the tenant left on 2027-02-10.
- Steps: on the lease page, set the date, click Record move-out. 1 click, 1 field, no new screen. The adjustment, the release to credit and the end of the lease all follow from that one action.
- A bug found here: the Charges page showed "No active leases to charge yet" and hid the Generate form once the only lease had ended. The final prorated month (TC-LEASE-12) could never be charged. The form now shows whenever any lease is active or ended.
- My mistake in a helper: `generateCharges` clicked Generate and returned at once, so a following `goto` sometimes cut the request off (TC-PAY-09 failed once for this). It now waits for the "… already existed" confirmation.
- The button is styled as a danger action and the hint says it can't be undone, because there's no way back.
- Remaining friction: a wrong move-out date is permanent. The fix would be a "correct move-out" action that adds adjustments in the other direction. No story asks for it; logged as OQ-22.
